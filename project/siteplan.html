Sweet Tooth

Website Planning Document for a family bakery project

W05 Project: Website Planning Document

This planning document outlines the design direction, audience needs, and visual style for the Sweet Tooth bakery website.

1. Site Name

Sweet Tooth

I chose the name Sweet Tooth because it is short, memorable, and immediately connected to desserts and baked treats. It gives the bakery a warm and friendly identity that fits families, celebrations, and special moments.

The cupcake logo supports the bakery brand by giving the site a simple visual identity that visitors can recognize quickly.

Optional domain: sweettoothbakery.cl

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of the Sweet Tooth website is to provide an attractive and easy-to-use online presence for a small bakery located in Graneros, Chile, serving customers in Rancagua and nearby areas through pickup and delivery.

The site will allow visitors to explore cakes, cupcakes, brownies, and other sweet treats, discover ideas for birthdays and family celebrations, estimate prices with a calculator, and place orders through a simple form. The website will also highlight popular products and make the bakery feel professional and trustworthy.

3. Scenarios

  • What cakes and treats are available for a birthday party, and how much would I need for 15 people?
  • Can I find ideas for a baby shower, family gathering, or another special celebration?
  • How can I customize a cake with a message and request delivery in Rancagua?
  • Which products are the most popular or best-selling at Sweet Tooth?

4. Color Schema

Soft Pastel Teal

#A8E6CF

Used for headings, buttons, links, borders, and accent areas.

Warm Cream

#F5F0E6

Used for section backgrounds, cards, and soft content areas.

White

#FFFFFF

Used as the main page background to keep the design clean and bright.

Dark Gray

#333333

Used for paragraph text to maintain strong readability and contrast.

5. Typography

Poppins

Used for headings, navigation, section titles, and buttons.

Roboto

Used for body text, descriptions, forms, and general page content.
